About Us

Allied Refreshment Distributing is a growing beverage distribution company based in Kansas City. We deliver to local businesses, restaurants, and retail locations throughout the metro area. We're looking for dependable, hardworking drivers to join our team.

What You'll Do

  • Drive a company vehicle on local delivery routes (home every night)
  • Load and unload product at customer locations
  • Build relationships with customers and provide excellent service
  • Maintain accurate delivery logs and paperwork
  • Perform basic vehicle inspections before and after routes

What We Offer

  • Competitive hourly pay with regular overtime opportunities
  • Consistent schedule — local routes, home every night
  • Paid training for the right candidate
  • Stable, full-time work — year-round position
  • Growth opportunities within the company
  • Supportive team environment

Requirements

  • Valid driver's license (CDL is a plus but NOT required)
  • Clean driving record
  • Must be able to lift 50+ lbs repeatedly throughout the day
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently
  • Must pass a background check and drug screening
  • Previous delivery or driving experience is a plus but not required — we will train
