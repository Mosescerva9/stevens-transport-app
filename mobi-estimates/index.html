How it works

A simple, organized estimating process

From document intake to a reviewed, bid-ready deliverable — without the overhead of an in-house department.

1

Submit Your Project

Upload the plans, specifications, addenda, bid forms, project location, required trades and bid deadline.

2

We Review the Scope

Our team reviews the documents, confirms the required services, identifies missing information and organizes the project.

3

Takeoff & Estimate

We prepare quantities, labor, materials, equipment, subcontractor costs and the requested bid breakdown.

4

Quality-Control Review

The estimate is checked for scope coverage, quantities, pricing, assumptions, exclusions, addenda and calculation accuracy.

5

Receive Your Estimate

The completed estimate, takeoff, marked-up plans and scope summary are uploaded to your client portal.

6

Request Revisions

Submit clarifications, revised plans, addenda or revision requests directly through the portal.
